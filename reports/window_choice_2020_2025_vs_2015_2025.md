## Interpretation

The 2020–2025 window is feasible and cleaner for contemporary trends. The 2015–2025 window is stronger for historical context, trend stability, and source/income comparisons. A good manuscript design is to use 2015–2025 as the main denominator and explicitly analyze 2020–2025 as a contemporary post-2020 subperiod; if journal scope demands a sharper contemporary framing, use 2020–2025 as primary and keep 2015–2019 as historical baseline.
